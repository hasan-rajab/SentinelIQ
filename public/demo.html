Evaluation protocol

Threshold chosen on validation only, then frozen before test scoring. Temporal ordering avoids random train/test leakage. The always-normal baseline gets high accuracy by missing every anomaly; positive-class precision, recall and F1 expose that failure.

Only the standalone metrics Isolation Forest was evaluated in this benchmark. The counts do not validate the autoencoder, BERT or multimodal ensemble and do not measure real incident outcomes.
